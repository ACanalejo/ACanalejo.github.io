Research Grants

Research grants and funded research projects I have contributed to, as Principal Investigator and otherwise.

As (Co-)Principal Investigator

UniLu FoKo Preparatory Funding (22,330 CHF ≈ 24,153€), with Alexander H. Trechsel (2026)
Preparatory funding for a project proposal on political socialization and school-based interventions to increase democratic engagement in Switzerland.
UniLu FaFoFö Funding (2,000 CHF ≈ 2,142€) (2025)
Survey experiment on the formation of devolution preferences in Italy.
Small Grant Aarhus University (35,000 DKK ≈ 4,686€), with Morgan Le Corre Juratic (2024)
Data collection on the consequences of losing against a highly disliked party in Austria.
UniLu FoKo Project Funding (12,231 CHF ≈ 13,101€), with Alberto López Ortega and Javier Padilla Moreno-Torres (2023)
Data collection on the formation of regional devolution preferences in Spain.
EUI Mission Funding for Research Activities (2,547€), with Morgan Le Corre Juratic (2022)
Data collection on the consequences of new party entry in France.

As Non-PI

  • CONSENT — Postdoctoral Researcher (September 2026 – present). ERC Consolidator Grant studying losers’ consent to electoral outcomes: what it means, what explains it, and what its consequences are.

  • DIGIPOL — Scientific Coordinator (June 2023 – August 2026), team member since 2022. SNSF-funded project to study the early politicization, or lack thereof, of digitalization issues from both the demand and supply sides, with a specific focus on Switzerland.

  • EU&I 2024 — Scientific Committee member (December 2023 – July 2024). EUI-based project that developed a cross-national Voting Advice Application for the 2024 European Parliament Election, reaching almost 2 million users; produced party- and user-level data and experimental results.

  • SOLID, POLCON, EUANDI2019, ¿Por qué odiamos la política?, Parlamentarios y Sociedad, ICOSOP — Country expert / research assistant, comparative politics and Spanish-politics projects since my undergraduate studies.
